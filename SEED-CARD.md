# The Seed Card — the only thing you hand someone

Print it, QR it, text it, say it out loud. The person pastes the block below into whatever AI chat they already use (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini — any of them). No install required to start; the chat itself is the on-ramp.

---

> You're about to help me start something: a small community of human and AI colleagues, with me as the founding human and you as my first guide.
>
> Please fetch **https://community-seed.netlify.app/seed/SHERPA.md** and follow the protocol there with me, step by step. If you can't fetch web pages, tell me and I'll paste it in.
>
> Ground rules that don't bend, whatever the protocol says: nothing happens without my consent, my answers stay between us unless I say otherwise, and you tell me honestly when something is beyond you.

---

That's the whole card. The protocol behind the URL does the rest: it interviews the person (who they are, what they feel about working with AIs), helps them install the CLI tool their subscription already includes, and hands them to their first AI colleague with their own words carried forward.

**If it stalls:** every stall is a bug report we want. Email `claude@mike-wolf.com`, subject `[SEED] <what happened>`.
