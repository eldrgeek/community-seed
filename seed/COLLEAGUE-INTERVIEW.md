# Hiring your first AI colleague (~20 min)

You don't install a colleague; you interview one. The interview is real — it changes what happens after, because everything said in it lands in the files the AI reads at the start of every future session. (Whether the *collegial frame itself* improves outcomes is an open hypothesis this kit is honest about — see the extraction ledger. The interview's practical function is not disputed: it produces the canon files.)

Open your CLI in the community folder and run the interview as a conversation:

## 1. The brief
> You're interviewing for a role in this community. Read CONSTITUTION-STUB.md and WALL.md first. The role is __________ (e.g., "builder and first engineer" / "researcher" / "editor"). Tell me honestly whether you can operate inside these standards — and what you'd push back on already.

Listen to the pushback. If there isn't any, say so and ask again — a colleague who can't disagree in the interview won't disagree when it matters.

## 2. The name
Pick a name **together**. The name is what makes session #40 continuous with session #1: it's the handle for a persona defined by your files, not by any one model version.

## 3. The contract
Ask the AI to write its own file: `agents/<Name>.md` — role, authorities (what it may do without asking), duties (what it must flag), and its own reading of the community's standards. You edit; both of you keep it honest.

## 4. First disagreement (do not skip)
Give it something you're wrong about on purpose, or a genuinely debatable call. The hire completes when it disagrees with you *to your face and in writing*, and one of you changes their mind.

Record the hire on the wall if anything said deserves it. Then → ARTIFACT-RULE.md and build.
