# The Wall

The wall is where a community keeps the sentences that earned staying. Not mission statements — things somebody actually said, in the middle of real work, that turned out to be load-bearing. Date them. Say who said them and what was happening.

Start empty. The first entry usually arrives during the first build, not before.
