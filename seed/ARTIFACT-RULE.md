# The artifact rule

1. **Done = a durable artifact at a path a stranger could open.** A file, a page, a running thing. Not a chat transcript.
2. Words about the artifact are not the artifact. Exit codes lie; "it should work" lies more.
3. The first build is small and *for a real person you can name* — a page for the block party, an intake form for the food bank, a guide for your mother's phone. Ship it the same day.
4. **Verify by looking**, not by asking the AI whether it worked. Open the page. Click the button. Then say what you saw.
5. If it's almost right, say "almost right" out loud — then fix it or record it. Never quietly ship it.
6. Keep the failure in the record. The build that broke twice before working is a *better* story and a better teacher than the polished summary.
7. Sign it, both of you: `artifacts/<name>/CREDITS.md` — what the human did, what the AI did, honestly.
8. Put one sentence on the wall if the build earned one.
9. The artifact is the evidence. (Everything above is this sentence, unrolled.)
