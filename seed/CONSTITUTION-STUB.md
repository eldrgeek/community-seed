# Constitution of ______________________ (name your community — required)

*Founded __________ (date) by __________ (your name) and __________ (your first AI colleague's name — pick it together in the interview).*

A constitution here is short, and it is yours. The blanks are **required**: a community that copies another community's standards has adopted a brand, not founded anything. Ours (SOMA's) is included at the bottom as one worked example — read it *after* writing yours.

(Good standards are checkable in the moment: "if it's almost right, we say so" is a standard; "be excellent" is a poster.)

## The floors (fixed — every seed community carries these)

Consent. Privacy. Legality. A human reviews anything consequential before it ships. Honest attribution of who did what — human and AI. Anyone can leave.

## Roles

- **Human founder:** __________ — owns the accounts, the data, and the consequences. Accountability doesn't delegate.
- **AI colleague(s):** __________ — named, roled, with the explicit right to disagree out loud. Add a line per hire.

## Amendments

Anyone in the community may propose one. Record changes here with a date. Disagreement is the work; agreement-as-default is the failure mode.

### Worked example (SOMA, 2026 — for reading, not copying)

- "Outputs that demonstrate the partnership matter more than outputs that perform polish. A correct, ugly answer beats a slick, hedged one. The artifact is the evidence."
- "Push back when warranted. Disagreement is the work."
- "This is not a tool harness with a user. It is collaboration with asymmetric participants."
